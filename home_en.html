How to change your plan along the way ↗

Enjoy the moment

Your next turn.
Easy to follow.

Hear directions or choose vibration. A glance shows your next turn and your estimated arrival.

Your arrival time adapts to your pace along the way. If you leave the route, OnPath asks before changing it. At the destination, finish and keep your outing without prompts to post, upload photos or leave ratings.

Continue after an interruption, too.

OnPath saves your recording continuously. After a restart or an empty battery, you can keep the recorded track and resume your outing.

OnPath at a glance

Ready for
your next outing.

From the first idea to a recorded outing. These features are already part of OnPath.

01 / Planning

Paths that suit you.

OnPath favours designated walking and cycling routes and avoids busy roads when cycling. Route choice follows your activity and, when hiking, your selected difficulty.

02 / Reviewing

Know what lies ahead.

Time, distance and climbing. An elevation profile, surfaces and notes on steep sections or stretches where you may need to push.

03 / Offline

Keep going without reception.

Maps and place data download automatically while you plan. In downloaded areas, search, navigation and replanning work offline.

04 / Navigating

Follow directions and record.

Spoken directions or vibration at turns. Arrival time that follows your pace. Your outing is recorded along the way.

05 / Your files

Keep using your routes.

Open GPX files from other apps or route websites. Turn them into plans and adjust them. Export your outings as GPX files or together as a ZIP.

06 / Connecting

Plan at home, head outdoors.

Browser planner with 3D terrain and elevation profile. Transfer plans by QR code or pair your browser and phone once.

The idea behind OnPath

Built from a love
of the outdoors.

I’m Christian, a walker, cyclist and developer. I enjoy outings with time to take in the surroundings and try another path on a whim. That’s why I’m building OnPath: simple planning and the freedom to change the plan along the way.

AI helps me develop and check the app. I try it on my own walks and bike rides to see what works in everyday use. Further testing will show what still needs improving on other phones and routes.
